How Quality Is Held

Four checkpoints between your drawing and the packed carton

1. Drawing review

Tolerance, material and finish are checked against the drawing before quoting. Features that cannot be produced as drawn are flagged with the alternative we can hold.

2. First article

The first piece is measured against the drawing and photographed before the batch continues. Nothing runs on until the first article is accepted.

3. In-process checks

Key dimensions are checked during the run, so a drift is corrected on the machine rather than discovered after the parts are packed.

4. Final check & packing

Appearance is checked part by part, quantities are counted, and the inspection photos go out with the shipping documents.

Where the Decision Sits

Measured values and photos are shared, the acceptance call is yours

How a dimension is judged is decided by your drawing, not by us. Where the drawing is open, we send the measured value and a photo of the part and ask you to confirm before the batch is released. If a part measures outside the drawing, it is reworked or replaced; it is not shipped with a note.
