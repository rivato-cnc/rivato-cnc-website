Injection Molding Questions

Answers before you send the drawing

Who owns the mold?
Mold design and specification are handled by our engineers, and the tool remains your asset. Terms are stated in the quotation before the tool is cut.

Are the parts made in your workshop?
Mold design and trial shots are managed by our engineers; the volume press runs at a vetted partner plant, inspected and documented before shipment.

Can you move a printed or cast design into molding?
Yes. A design proven by printing or vacuum casting carries over to molding with the mold built for volume, which keeps the appearance consistent.
