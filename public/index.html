Our Manufacturing Capabilities

Six processes, one point of contact — from prototype to series

CNC Machining

3-, 4- and 5-axis milling and turning in aluminum, stainless steel, brass, copper and engineering plastics. Prototypes to series runs; as tight as ±0.005 mm on selected features.

CNC machining details →

3D Printing

SLA and SLS printing in plastic and metal — prototypes, functional test parts and master patterns for low-volume casting.

3D printing details →

Sheet Metal Fabrication

Laser cutting, punching and bending from standard sheet gauges. Flat patterns are checked before cutting and the first article is inspected against your drawing.

Sheet metal details →

Vacuum Casting

Small-batch parts cast from silicone molds made on a printed or machined master, with a surface finish close to an injection-molded part.

Injection Molding

Questions Buyers Ask First

Short answers, before you send the drawing

Which processes do you run?
Six: CNC machining (3-, 4- and 5-axis milling and turning), SLA/SLS 3D printing, sheet metal fabrication, vacuum casting, injection molding and surface finishing — quoted under one contact.

What files should I send for a quotation?
STEP, IGES, DWG or PDF, together with material, quantity, tolerance and surface finish. Mark the critical features on the drawing — we confirm them before cutting.

What is the minimum order quantity?
One piece for prototypes. Series quantities are quoted per drawing; unit price follows material, tolerance and finishing.

How fast do you reply?
Within 4 to 8 working hours, Monday to Friday, Shenzhen time (UTC+8).

Do you ship to Europe and North America?
Yes — air and sea freight, with export documentation handled in house. Road and rail are also available for Central Asia and Europe.
