Packing and Shipping

From the last inspection to your door

Packing

Each part goes into its own bag, then EPE foam and a double-wall carton. Heavy or fragile parts ship in a wooden crate. Labels carry part number, quantity and lot.

Air express and air freight

DHL, FedEx and UPS for samples and small batches; consolidated air freight when the weight makes express uneconomic.

Sea freight

For larger batches, sea freight from Shenzhen with the forwarder of your choice, or ours if you prefer.

Documents

Commercial invoice, packing list and inspection photos go out with every shipment. A certificate of origin is issued on request.

Shipping Terms

What is stated on the quotation and the documents

IncotermEXW or FOB Shenzhen, stated on the quotation
FreightQuoted separately from the part price, at cost
