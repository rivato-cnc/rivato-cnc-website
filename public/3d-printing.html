3D Printing Questions

Answers before you send the drawing

What is 3D printing used for here?
Form and fit checks, functional test parts, presentation models, and master patterns that silicone molds are cast from.

Can printed parts be used as production parts?
Printed parts are normally used to prove a design. Once the design is fixed, production moves to vacuum casting, injection molding or CNC machining, depending on quantity and material.

Which file formats do you accept?
STEP, IGES, STL, DWG or PDF. Tell us the material and the features that matter, and we confirm what the print can and cannot hold.
