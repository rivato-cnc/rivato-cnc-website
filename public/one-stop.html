Why one thread beats six suppliers

Most delays sit between suppliers, not inside them

A part is rarely just a part. The drawing has to be manufacturable, the tooling has to match the drawing, the finish has to match the sample, the packaging has to protect the finish, and the assembly has to fit what was packed. Hand those steps to five companies and every hand-off becomes your problem: a tolerance nobody re-checked, a dieline that arrives after the cartons were printed, a kit missing one screw.

We keep those steps in one thread — one contact, one schedule, one inspection standard. Where a step is outside what we do ourselves, we say so before you order, produce it with a partner we already work with, and inspect it before it leaves.

What we take on

Eight stages, from an idea to a carton on your dock

1 · Design & DFM

CAD modelling from sketches, samples or 2D prints, drawings for tolerance, material and process selection, and a manufacturability review before anything is cut.

2 · Prototype

CNC, SLA/SLS printing and vacuum casting for form, fit and function samples. A prototype can later become a master pattern or an assembly fixture.

3 · Tooling

Soft tooling for low volume, mould design review, jigs and fixtures for repeatable assembly, and gauges for the features that matter.

4 · Production

CNC machining to ±0.005 mm on specified features, sheet metal to ±0.1 mm, injection moulding, and partner capacity when volume outgrows our floor.

5 · Finishing

Anodizing, sandblasting, powder coating, plating and laser marking, matched to your approved sample or a stated RAL — the colour call stays with you.

6 · Packaging

Retail-ready boxes, foam or pulp inserts, ESD bags, labels and barcodes. We work to your artwork, or prepare a dieline for your approval before printing.

7 · Assembly & Kitting

Sub-assembly, screw fastening, gluing, basic functional checks, polybagging, and kits packed per SKU or per order with carton marks.

8 · Logistics

Consolidation of multi-supplier orders, EXW / FOB / DDP terms, air, sea and express, plus the export paperwork that goes with them.

Scope, stated plainly

What we do ourselves and what we arrange

Machining, sheet metal, surface finishing, assembly and packing are done with our own equipment and people in Bao'an, Shenzhen. 3D printing master patterns, silicone moulds and injection tooling are produced with long-standing partners whose output we inspect before it reaches you.

We do not claim certificates we do not hold, and we do not promise work we have never shipped. If a project needs something outside our scope — a specific plating line, a medical cleanroom, a certification we do not carry — we will say so at quotation, not after the PO.
