Who We Are

A Shenzhen precision shop working with European and North American buyers

Rivato CNC is the international trading name of Shenzhen Ruiwatuo Precision Machinery Technology Co., Ltd., a precision manufacturing company based in Bao'an District, Shenzhen. The company was registered in January 2026 and works with buyers in Europe and North America on prototypes and series parts.

Six processes are coordinated by one team: CNC machining, 3D printing, sheet metal fabrication, vacuum casting, injection molding and surface finishing. Drawings are reviewed before quoting, first articles are inspected before a batch run, and parts are shipped by air or sea with the inspection record attached.

Quotations are prepared from STEP, IGES, DWG or PDF files with material, quantity, tolerance and finish stated. Enquiries are answered within 4–8 working hours, Monday to Friday.
