How a Quote Is Prepared

Four steps from your file to a price

1

Send the drawing

STEP, IGES, DWG or PDF, with material, quantity, tolerance and finish. A photo of a similar part helps where the drawing is open.

2

DFM check

Features that cannot be produced as drawn are listed with the alternative we can hold, before any price is given.

3

Price and lead time

Returned by email or WhatsApp with unit price, tooling cost where relevant, lead time and Incoterm.

4

First article

The first piece is measured and photographed; the batch runs after you accept it.

What We Need From You

The fields that decide the price

File formatSTEP, IGES, DWG, DXF or PDF; native CAD files are welcome
MaterialAluminium, stainless steel, brass, copper, titanium or engineering plastic
QuantityOne piece or a series run; the quantity asked for is the quantity quoted
ToleranceAs marked on the drawing; as tight as ±0.005 mm on selected features
FinishAnodizing, plating, sandblasting or powder coating, with colour where it matters
DeliveryDestination city and post code, so the freight can be quoted with the parts
