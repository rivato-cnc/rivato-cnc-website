Laser Cutting

Profiles and openings cut from standard sheet gauges, with the flat pattern checked against your drawing before the sheet is cut.

Punching

Holes, slots and cut-outs punched for repeatable series work, with tooling matched to the feature size.

Bending

Folds and brackets formed to the angles on your drawing, with the first article measured before the run continues.

Sheet Metal Fabrication Questions

Answers before you send the drawing

How do you check the flat pattern?
The flat pattern is reviewed against your drawing before the sheet is cut, so openings, bend lines and material grain are confirmed first. The first article is then measured before the run continues.

Which thicknesses can you run?
Standard sheet gauges. Send the thickness on the drawing and we confirm what is available before quoting.

Can you supply finished enclosures?
Yes. Cut, punched and bent sheets are assembled where the drawing calls for it, then finished and packed for export.
